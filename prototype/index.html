How it works

1

Enter your destination

Any address in LA. If it's inside a ride region, you'll see trips headed to that region.

2

Join or create

Join solo or as a party, up to 6 per ride. If a trip is full, extra sign-ups start a new listing.

3

Meet at pickup

Gather at the Village, EVK, or Webb at departure time. Use party chat if plans change.

4

Ride, split, walk

The booker pays and adds everyone to the split. The Uber drops you at the region center and you walk from there.

The honor system

No-shows leave everyone else paying more. Miss a ride you signed up for and you get a strike. Three strikes and you're suspended for a while.

Cancelling within 2 hours of departure carries a cancellation fee, which you agree to when you sign up.
